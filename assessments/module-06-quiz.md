# Module 6 quiz

Nine scenario questions about indexing your own files locally: what the system does, what it costs
on a CPU, what you should point it at, and how you find out whether it is working. Several are
about failures that look like a bad model and are not.

**Pass mark: 70%.** You need 7 of 9. This module is optional; skipping it removes lesson 10.4 and
one capstone criterion, and nothing else.
