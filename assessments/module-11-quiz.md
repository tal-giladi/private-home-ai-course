# Module 11 quiz

Nine scenario questions about who can reach your stack, what they get if they do, how you reach it
from outside, and what you do when an advisory turns up. Several of them describe a stack that is
almost secure, and the answer is the one that survives being wrong for a month.

**Pass mark: 70%.** You need 7 of 9.

Review the module if you need it:

- [11.1 · Threat-model your home AI stack](../lessons/module-11/lesson-01.md)
- [11.2 · Bind addresses and ports](../lessons/module-11/lesson-02.md)
- [11.3 · Remote access the safe way](../lessons/module-11/lesson-03.md)
- [11.4 · Updates, CVEs and model supply chain](../lessons/module-11/lesson-04.md)

The two checklists this module fills in are the [security
checklist](../templates/security-checklist.md) and the [update routine](../templates/update-routine.md).
The [capstone rubric](capstone-rubric.md) is graded against both of them.
