# Module 2 quiz

Nine scenario questions about why a machine is fast or slow, what it costs in memory, how to measure
it properly, and how to turn all three into one decision. These are the questions that decide which
model the rest of the course will use on your hardware.

**Pass mark: 70%.** You need 7 of 9. The explanations are the point: most of them explain a limit
rather than a flag, so a wrong answer is usually a sign of a wrong mental model rather than a
misremembered name.

Before you start, fill in the three numbers that these questions assume you have: your peak memory
bandwidth, your measured efficiency factor, and the generation rate you measured for one model.
They belong in the [generation and prompt speed table](../templates/measure-yours.md), and every
question below is easier once they are written down.
