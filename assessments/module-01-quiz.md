# Module 1 quiz

Nine scenario questions about installing a model runtime, reading what a model is called, and the
settings that decide both the answer and the memory it costs. Most of them describe a situation
that looks like a fault and is actually a decision somebody made earlier.

**Pass mark: 70%.** You need 7 of 9. Every wrong answer here has a reason attached to it, and the
[Glossary](../glossary.md) links each term to the lesson that introduces it.

Before you start, it helps to have three numbers from your own machine: the size of the model you
pulled, the generation rate you measured, and the process memory while it was loaded. Those three
appear in several of the questions below, and they belong in your
[measure-yours table](../templates/measure-yours.md).
