# Module 0 quiz

Nine scenario questions about what a private home AI stack is, what your machine can hold, and
what `homelab.py doctor` is telling you. None of them asks you to recall a command: every question
is a situation you could actually meet in the first week of building this.

**Pass mark: 70%.** You need 7 of 9. Where an answer is wrong, the explanation usually contains the
reason, and the [Glossary](../glossary.md) links every term to the lesson that introduces it.

Before you start, it is worth having your own `doctor` output in front of you: the tier, the
memory channel count and the model it recommended are the inputs to most of these questions, and
they are also the first rows of your [measure-yours table](../templates/measure-yours.md).
