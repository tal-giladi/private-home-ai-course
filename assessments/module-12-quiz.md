# Module 12 quiz

Nine scenario questions about running the stack for real: the memory budget, what to keep resident,
what to back up, how to prove nothing leaves the house, and what changes when Home Assistant moves
to its own machine. Several describe a stack that works perfectly until something goes wrong at 3
a.m., which is the situation this module exists for.

**Pass mark: 70%.** You need 7 of 9.

Review the module if you need it:

- [12.1 · Always-on without starving the voice pipeline](../lessons/module-12/lesson-01.md)
- [12.2 · Backups and a restore drill](../lessons/module-12/lesson-02.md)
- [12.3 · Prove there is no cloud](../lessons/module-12/lesson-03.md)
- [12.4 · Your always-on home](../lessons/module-12/lesson-04.md)

The two artefacts this module produces are a completed restore drill and a recorded no-cloud test.
Both are graded in the [capstone rubric](capstone-rubric.md), and both are worth more as evidence
than as intentions.
