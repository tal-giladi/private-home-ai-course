# Washing Machine — Model WM-2200 — Instruction Manual

Manufacturer: Northline Appliances. Document revision 3, published 2025-02-11.
This document is fictional and exists only for the Private Home AI course.

## 1. Safety

Do not open the machine while it is powered. Do not use an extension cord. The machine must be
connected to a grounded outlet. If the drum makes a loud grinding noise, stop using it and contact
support; continuing to run it can damage the bearings.

Children must not be left alone with a running cycle. The door stays locked for the first three
minutes of a cycle and for two minutes after it ends.

## 2. Installation

Minimum clearance: 60 mm at the back, 100 mm at each side, 120 mm above. Leave room in front for
the door to open through 180 degrees. The machine must sit level; check with a spirit level and
adjust the front feet until it does not rock.

The transit bolts at the back must be removed before first use. Leaving them in will damage the
suspension and is not covered by the warranty.

Water inlet: 3/4 inch BSP. Maximum inlet pressure 10 bar. Drain hose maximum reach 1.5 m.

## 3. Daily use

Choose a programme with the dial, add detergent to compartment II, softener to compartment IV, and
press Start. The main programmes are:

- **Cotton** — 2 hours 10 minutes at 40 degrees. For everyday cotton laundry.
- **Synthetics** — 1 hour 25 minutes at 40 degrees. Use less detergent than cotton.
- **Delicates** — 45 minutes at 30 degrees. For wool and silk.
- **Quick 30** — 30 minutes at 30 degrees. For lightly soiled loads only.
- **Spin only** — 12 minutes. Dries nothing; it removes water.
- **Rinse and Spin** — 32 minutes.
- **Drum clean** — 60 minutes, empty, with detergent in compartment II. Run it every three months.

Load no more than 7 kg of cotton or 3.5 kg of wool. Overloading is the most common cause of a
washing machine that will not spin.

## 4. The "it will not drain" procedure

1. Close the water tap.
2. Open the filter flap at the bottom right of the front panel.
3. Place a shallow tray under it, because roughly 1 litre of water will come out.
4. Turn the filter counter-clockwise. Do not force it.
5. Remove the obstruction. Coins and hair grips are the usual cause.
6. Refit the filter, close the flap, and run **Rinse and Spin**.

If water still collects, the pump is blocked. Step 3 above is the whole fix; anything beyond it is
a technician's job.

## 5. Error codes

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Door not locked | Check nothing is caught in the door seal. Retry. |
| E2 | Water not filling within 5 minutes | Check the tap is open and the inlet hose is not kinked. |
| E3 | Water not draining | Follow section 4. |
| E4 | Door did not unlock | Wait two minutes after the cycle ends, then retry. |
| E5 | Motor over-temperature | Let the machine cool for 30 minutes. Repeated E5 needs a technician. |
| E6 | Unbalanced load | Redistribute the laundry and run Spin only. |
| E10 | Communication fault | Unplug for 5 minutes and replug. If it returns, call support. |

## 6. Maintenance

Clean the filter every three months. Run Drum clean every three months. Leave the door ajar when
not in use. Do not use the machine to wash anything other than laundry and textiles.
