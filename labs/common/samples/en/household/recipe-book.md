## Weeknight pasta

Serves 2. Boil plenty of water and salt it properly — it should taste of the sea.

- 200 g dried spaghetti
- 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 60 ml olive oil
- 1 red chilli, seeds out
- 50 g parmesan, grated
- A handful of flat-leaf parsley

Cook the pasta for one minute less than the packet says. Meanwhile warm the oil gently and let the
garlic go pale, not brown. Add the chilli for the last thirty seconds off the heat. Drain the pasta,
keep a cup of the water, and toss everything together off the heat with the parmesan. The pasta
water is the sauce; do not skip it.

## Lentil soup

Serves 4. Freezes well.

- 250 g brown lentils, rinsed
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery sticks, diced
- 1 litre stock
- 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Olive oil, salt, a lemon at the end

Sweat the vegetables in oil for ten minutes without browning. Add everything else, simmer 35
minutes, blend half of it for thickness. Lemon off the heat, always.

## A very good cake

- 220 g butter, softened
- 220 g sugar
- 4 eggs
- 220 g self-raising flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- Zest of 2 lemons

Cream butter and sugar for five minutes; it should go pale. Eggs one at a time. Fold in the flour
last. 180 °C, 35 minutes. It is done when a skewer comes out with a couple of moist crumbs, not
clean.
