Guest Wi-Fi
===========

Network:  Guest-2G
Password: welcome-in-the-summer

This network is isolated from the home network. Guests can reach the internet; they cannot reach
the router admin panel, the Home Assistant instance, or the AI machine on 192.168.1.50.

House wifi: Brighton-5G / purple-otter-42
Please do not hand this one out. If you need it, ask.

If the guest network is slow, it is because everything IoT is on it by design. Do not move smart
bulbs back onto the house network to make it faster - that undoes the isolation and puts a
firmware bug three devices away from your files.
